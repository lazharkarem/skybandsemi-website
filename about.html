Our mission

Specialist expertise, applied where it creates most value.

SkyBand Semiconductors is an RF and mmWave design company focused on integrated circuit development and high-frequency systems.

We work at the point where architecture, circuit performance and physical implementation meet. Our role ranges from solving a specific design challenge to carrying a complete programme through silicon and hardware validation.

Every engagement begins with a clear understanding of what you need to achieve — at system level, at circuit level, and at the measurement bench — and we remain connected to that goal throughout.
