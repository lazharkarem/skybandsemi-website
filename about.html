About SkyBand

RF engineering connected to the complete product.

Our mission

Specialist expertise, applied at the right point.

SkyBand Semiconductors is an RF and mmWave design company focused on integrated circuit development and high-frequency systems.

We work at the point where architecture, circuit performance and physical implementation meet. Our role can range from solving a specific design challenge to carrying a complete programme through silicon and hardware validation.

Every engagement begins with a clear understanding of what you need to achieve — at system level, circuit level and validation level — and we remain connected to that goal throughout.
