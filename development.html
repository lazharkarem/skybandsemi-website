Process overview

11 steps across 4 phases.

Complete development ownership means every design decision made in phase one is preserved and carried through to the measurement bench in phase four.

DEFINE Steps 01–02
DESIGN Steps 03–07
REALIZE Steps 08–10
VALIDATE Step 11
One team, end to end

No knowledge gaps. No costly handoffs. A single engineering partner accountable for every deliverable.

01
DEFINE System Requirements

Capture and formalise the complete RF system requirements — frequency, power, gain, noise, linearity, environment and interface constraints.

02
DEFINE Architecture Definition

Define the system signal chain, technology platform, and architectural partitioning. Evaluate feasibility and key risk items.

03
DESIGN Circuit Design

Transistor-level schematic design and hand analysis. Initial sizing for gain, noise, linearity and power consumption targets.

04
DESIGN Circuit Simulation

Comprehensive simulation across process, voltage and temperature (PVT) corners. Large-signal, small-signal and noise characterization.

05
DESIGN EM Simulation

3D electromagnetic modelling of critical passives, interconnects and substrate structures to capture parasitic effects.

06
DESIGN Layout

Full custom physical layout of the circuit. Optimized for performance, manufacturability and design rule compliance.

07
DESIGN DRC / LVS Verification

Design Rule Check and Layout vs. Schematic sign-off. Post-layout parasitic extraction and re-simulation.

08
REALIZE Tape-Out

Preparation and submission of final GDS-II layout data to the foundry for silicon manufacture.

09
REALIZE Packaging

Die assembly into the target package — QFN, flip-chip, bare-die, or module depending on application requirements.

10
REALIZE PCB Evaluation Board

Design of a dedicated test board for RF measurement — matched, accessible, and optimised for characterisation.

11
VALIDATE Measurement & Validation

Full RF characterisation — S-parameters, noise, linearity, power, and system-level performance against specification.
