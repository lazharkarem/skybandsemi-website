Best-in-class RF switch performance. High integration density enables complex front-end solutions in a single die. Cost-effective at volume production.

02 / SiGe BiCMOS

Silicon Germanium

Exceptional fT and fmax for mmWave analog and mixed-signal design. Combines high-frequency bipolar transistors with CMOS digital circuitry in one process.

Key parameters
FrequencyUp to 300+ GHz
StrengthLow noise, mmWave
ApplicationsRadar, satcom, 5G
LNAmmWaveMixed-signal

Gallium Nitride

Wide bandgap semiconductor with high breakdown voltage and superior power density. Ideal for demanding microwave power amplification in radar and defence systems.

Key parameters
FrequencyDC – 100 GHz
StrengthPower density
ApplicationsRadar, defence, PA
