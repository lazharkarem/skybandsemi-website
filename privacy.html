Privacy Policy

How SkyBand Semiconductors collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last Updated: September 2026  ·  SkyBand Semiconductors, 12 Rue des Moissons, 14850 Escoville, France

1. Who we are

SkyBand Semiconductors (the "Company") is the data controller responsible for your personal data. We are an RF and mmWave integrated circuit engineering company headquartered at 12 Rue des Moissons, 14850 Escoville, France. You can reach us at contact@skybandsemi.com for any data-related queries.

2. Data we collect

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we collect:

Identity Data
First name, last name, or similar identifier you provide in the form.
Contact Data
Email address provided in the enquiry form.
Professional Data
Company name, project type, frequency range, and project description you submit.
Technical Data
IP address, browser type, and standard server log data collected automatically on site visits.

3. How we use your data

We use your personal data solely to:

  • Respond to your direct enquiry and provide the information or proposal you requested.
  • Evaluate your project requirements and determine whether we can assist.
  • Comply with any applicable legal or regulatory obligation.

We do not use your data for marketing without your explicit prior consent. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Data retention

We retain enquiry data for a period of 36 months from the date of receipt, after which it is securely deleted, unless a longer retention period is required by law or by an ongoing business relationship.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

Right to access your personal data
Right to rectification of inaccurate data
Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
Right to restrict processing
Right to data portability
Right to object to processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@skybandsemi.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data transfers.
